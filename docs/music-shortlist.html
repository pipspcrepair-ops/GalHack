Tick keep on anything you like and add a note. Ticks and notes are saved in this browser (localStorage), so they survive a reload. Only one track plays at a time. Press "Copy my picks" and paste the result into chat.

Generated music is the only music. Every sample here comes from the game's own drone generator, no audio files. Each world rolls its own root, scale, pace, instruments and theme; each place, biome, climate, star, tech level and time of day changes the colour around that. Most run 90 seconds, the Hollow ones 3 minutes because it builds slowly, and one runs 12 minutes to show the long shape, quiet included. Your first ten are kept at the bottom. What I could not verify

  • I cannot hear anything. Mood notes are the authors' words.
  • Lengths are not shown on most OpenGameArt pages. The bold player lengths come from the player's metadata during a short play test, and some are shorter previews of the real file.
  • Whether the OpenGameArt and incompetech players work when this file is opened from disk depends on those servers allowing hotlinking. The "Open page" link always works.
  • YouTube embeds (itch.io rows) can refuse to play inside a page opened from disk; the page link is the fallback.
  • OpenGameArt previews are sometimes shorter MP3 conversions of the real file; the real file is on the page.
  • The author field on OpenGameArt is the uploader's name. I checked descriptions for remix, sample, cover or "based on" wording and found none except where a row says so, but I did not trace every track's origin.
  • Akiwaya "Space Electronic Music" and the JDSherbert Ambiences pack from the earlier shortlist were not re-checked on this pass, so they are not listed.
