# Where a war's blasts came down: the strikes of a nuclear exchange or a bombardment, and what they
# aimed at. Read by StrikePlan (src/Gen/Strikes.cs) for a world whose fall (or scar) a strike names.
# Nothing here names a place: a strike goes on whatever the world's plan built that matches a target.
# The map's craters and the ruined places are the same blasts, so they always agree.
#
#   strike <id>            how one kind of war came down
#     falls ids            the fall layers (data/world-layers.txt) that bring it
#     count lo hi          strikes on a 16x8 km world, from the mildest fall to the hardest (the roll's
#                          severity, 0 to 1); a bigger world takes more, a scar in one region a third
#     crater lo hi         a ground burst's crater radius, metres (the bigger the target, the bigger the yield)
#     depth d  rim r       the bowl's depth below the land and its thrown rim, as shares of the radius
#     ruin dead fade m [+] how far out it flattened, burnt the land dead (ash, nothing growing) and
#                          faded back to the land's own: m times the crater radius, plus metres (a big
#                          blast's rings in metres: multiples of a 250 m bowl would kill the whole world)
#     centre yes           a ground burst comes down in the target itself (the city it hit), not beside it
#     flatten yes          the places round it grade by distance: foundations and tower stumps by the rim,
#                          stumps a storey or two tall, gutted shells, then standing ruins further out
#     rad n [aged]         radiation at ground zero, RAD, to none at the dead ring's edge (warned there),
#                          and the share of it a long century takes away
#     field lo hi          shell holes in its crater field on a 16x8 km world, mildest to hardest fall: in
#                          barrages round the targets, along the fronts between them and down their roads
#     shell lo hi          a shell hole's radius, metres, most of them near lo
#     air share            strikes that burst in the air: no crater, the same flattening and burning
#     miss share           strikes that missed: down within spread of the target, never in open water
#     spread lo hi         how far off a miss came down, metres
#     wild share           the odd one over open country, aimed at nothing (an airburst)
#     salvo lo hi step m   each strike a stick of lo to hi craters in a line, step metres (and 3.5 radii) apart
#     era L M H            how many strikes the old world could throw, by its tech level
#     aged depth rim dead  what a long century since takes off the bowl, the rim and the dead ground (past 1:
#                          the land has all grown back before the century is out)
#
#   target <kind>          what a strike aims at; the first that matches a place wins
#     match words          any of: tier.T tag.T id.T role.T (the plan's role) primary capital plan.city
#                          infra.bridge infra.junction route.K; a+b needs both
#     value n              what it was worth a strike (0: never aimed at)
#     takes n              most strikes one target draws
#     share s              most of a world's strikes this kind draws (its roads never take most of a war)
#     hard yes             hardened: always a ground burst
#     air share            its own share of airbursts (cities and towns: the blast over the streets)
#     naval yes            a ship target: its strike may come down on the water
#     within yes           lived in: a strike that comes down in its target (centre yes) comes down in its middle
#     era L M H            its worth by the old world's tech level
