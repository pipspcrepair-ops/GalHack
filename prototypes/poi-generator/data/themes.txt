# Placement themes: what usually turns up on a kind of world (its themed set, weighted), and the
# share of outliers, places from outside the set that don't belong there. Outliers are rarer and
# lean toward the ominous and the rare; every planet gets at least one, never more than the max.
#
#   theme <id>
#     match     archetype[.variant] names it applies to (temperate, arid.canyon, ice.glacier...),
#               or fallen (a post-collapse world), or default
#     density   POIs per square kilometre
#     tech      the world's tech level (low, mid, high): families with layouts per tech level pick theirs
#     outliers  min max   the share of a planet's POIs that are outliers
#     set       family weight ...
