# ---- fences: a part a street chooses, like the parts of its houses ----
# A street takes one or two kinds most of its lots have, from its families' fence lines weighted by how well each
# kind suits its weather, tech, purse and age; now and then a lot has an odd one out, and some lots have none. The
# next street, or a better-off or poorer neighbourhood of the same town, picks again. A fence follows its lot's own
# lines, meets at corners on one post, opens only for the path and the drive (gate posts either side, the gate
# standing open) and is drawn in the player's shapes: thin panels between posts, walls, hedges, railings on a low
# wall, or edging laid flat. Lines:
#   fencekind <id>         starts one
#   name <words>
#   body panel|wall|hedge|railing|edging
#   tall <cells>           1 waist-high, 2 a screen or wall over your head, 3 a compound wall (edging lies flat)
#   stuff <material> [<posts' material>]   timber steel alloy rust concrete ceramic rock plant resin cloth
#   paint <list>           white cream trim paint dark black stone brick render wall steel galv rust green
#                          darkgreen olive bamboo wicker reed adobe silver grey wood (the street's own shades)
#   post pillar|rib|pier|none [<every n m>]   posts (or a wall's piers) along it as well as at its ends and gates
#   climate, tech, era, weight as for families; wealth <min> <max>: the purse that builds it
#   enclose <chance>       it runs right round the plot; else along the front and down the side to the house
