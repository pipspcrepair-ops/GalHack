# World dressing: what each layer value leaves lying about in a world's streets, squares, gardens and roads
# (docs/DESIGN.md section 8, "Every world is dressed by its layers"). Fields split by ' | '.
#
#   part <id> | model <builder> | style <word> | at <sites> | every <m> | chance <share> | far <m> | reach <m> | solid yes | only <tag>
#     at      span (across a street, between two posts), kerb (beside a street), lane (in a street, one side,
#             the other kept clear), square (open ground at a town's crossings), garden (a house's front),
#             wall (a house's front wall by its door), verge (beside a road or highway out of town),
#             road (in its lane, one side), field (open ground off a road), gate (a place's way in)
#     every   metres between them along a way at a strength of one; chance: the share of spots that take one
#     far     how far from a settlement it has thinned to a third
#     reach   how far it reaches round its spot: nothing else is set down inside it
#     only    a garden, wall or gate of a building with that family tag only (shops' awnings and signs)
#
#   lean <selector> | <part> <strength> ...
#     selector  layer.id (theme.parade_ground, fall.plague, moment.festival, marks.two_flags, creed.stampers...),
#               tag.word (a dress tag a rolled value carries), group.word (a fall's group), all (every settled world)
#     A world's strength for a part is every lean that holds added up, at most 2.5: how thick it lies.
#     Creeds' and presences' leans are the living's: kept fresh, and in the era they live in now.
#
# Every part is built in code from shapes, painted in the world's paint and worn by its age, and drawn by
# its era: timber, rope and oil on a low world, steel and sodium on a mid one, composite and light strips
# on a high one. Signs carry symbols only: there is no writing anywhere.
