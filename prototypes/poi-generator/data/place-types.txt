# Place types: what a kind of place has, each thing's real size range in metres, and how the things relate.
# No positions and no layouts: the planner (src/Gen/Arch/PlaceTypes.cs) rolls every spawn's own sketch from
# these, from its seed and its world's size, so no two spawns of a type are the same place.
#
#   type <id>
#     name     what it is called
#     tech     the tech levels it is built at
#     share    the most of its world's area it may take (the planner shortens the long parts toward
#              their lower ends to fit, and says so when even those don't)
#     part <id> <kind> [what] [sizes] [relation]...
#       kinds     surface <cover>      levelled made ground, laid like the world's roads (tarmac, concrete, gravel, dirt)
#                 building <program>   a building of data/programs.txt, sized here, with its rooms planned at build
#                 tanks | lights | fence | road <cover>
#       sizes     len w depth h r count cab shoulder concourse: a..b is rolled by each spawn
#       relations along <part> gap a..b      parallel to it, its centre line gap metres off, either side
#                 between <a> <b>            across the ground from one to the other (links)
#                 beside <part> [away <q>]   off a long side of it (the side away from q), touching or gap a..b off
#                 ends <part>                in line off each end of it
#                 faces <part>               its front (doors, cab) turned to it
#                 near <part> a..b, far <part> a..           how far from it (edge to edge)
#                 sees <part>                nothing taller stands between it and that part
#                 from <part>                a road from it out to the place's gate
#                 round a..b                 round everything, that far out
#
# Figures are real ones: ICAO Annex 14 (runway code 4: 45 m wide, shoulders to 60 m; taxiway code E 23 m;
# runway to parallel taxiway centre lines 168 to 190 m), FAA AC 150/5300-13 (aprons, hangar clearances),
# approach lighting 420 m (simple) to 900 m (full); regional towers 30 to 50 m, hangar spans 45 to 60 m.

type airport
  name Airport
  tech mid high
  share 0.05
  part runway   surface tarmac   len 1500..2500 w 45 shoulder 7.5
  part taxiway  surface tarmac   w 23 along runway gap 168..190
  part link     surface tarmac   w 23 count 3..5 between runway taxiway
  part apron    surface concrete len 300..600 depth 150..250 beside taxiway away runway
  part terminal building terminal len 120..200 depth 30..60 h 12..20 concourse 6 beside apron away taxiway faces apron
  part tower    building control_tower len 10..14 depth 10..14 h 30..50 cab 8..12 beside apron away taxiway near terminal 20..250 sees runway
  part hangar   building hangar count 2..5 len 45..60 depth 45..70 h 15..22 beside apron away taxiway faces apron gap 0..8
  part fuel     tanks count 3..6 r 6..12 h 8..14 beside taxiway away runway far terminal 300..
  part carpark  surface tarmac   len 80..200 depth 60..120 beside terminal away apron gap 8..20
  part access   road tarmac      w 7..10 from carpark
  part lights   lights           len 420..900 ends runway
  part fence    fence            h 2..3 round 40..80

# Spaceports by starport class, from the same planner: the game's own ships land and lift off vertically, so a port
# is a row of pads, each in its blast apron with a blast clearance to the next, a taxiway along them with a link out
# from each, an apron, the halls the ships go into through doors they fit, a terminal, a control tower that sees the
# pads, the fuel and oxidiser stores well apart and off the terminal, a car park, an access road and a perimeter.
# No strip: a type names one only where its craft need it. The world's class picks the biggest it may be, its size the
# biggest that fits (5% of its area): the smallest worlds get a landing field, never a shrunken starport.
# Figures: a launch complex is 1 to 3 km across (Kennedy LC-39A's pad perimeter about 1 km, the launch control centre
# 5 km off); SpaceX landing zones LZ-1 and LZ-2 are 86 m circles about 300 m apart for an 18 m leg span; LC-39A keeps its
# liquid oxygen and liquid hydrogen spheres (about 3,400 and 3,200 m3, near 19 m across) on opposite sides of the pad,
# some 400 m apart (NFPA 55 and DoD 6055.09 separate oxidiser from fuel and both from inhabited buildings); the
# Vehicle Assembly Building's doors are 139 m tall. This game's Small class runs about 10 to 30 m, its Large class
# to about 120 m: pads two to three times the craft (40 to 60 m small, 100 to 160 m large), blast aprons 10 to 40 m.
type starport
  name Starport
  tech high
  class a
  share 0.05
  part pads     pads concrete count 5..8 side 100..160 apron 30..40 gap 250..400
  part taxiway  surface concrete w 30..40 along pads gap 220..300
  part link     surface concrete w 30..40 between pads taxiway
  part apron    surface concrete len 600..1000 depth 220..320 beside taxiway away pads
  part terminal building terminal len 160..240 depth 40..60 h 14..20 concourse 6 beside apron away taxiway faces apron
  part tower    building control_tower len 14..18 depth 14..18 h 40..60 cab 10..14 beside apron away taxiway near terminal 40..300 sees pads
  part hangar   building hangar count 3..5 len 140..200 depth 120..160 h 50..70 beside apron away taxiway faces apron gap 10..30
  part fuel     tanks count 4..6 r 8..12 h 12..18 beside taxiway away pads far terminal 500..
  part oxidiser tanks count 3..5 r 8..12 h 12..18 beside taxiway away pads far fuel 400.. far terminal 500..
  part carpark  surface tarmac   len 150..300 depth 80..150 beside terminal away apron gap 10..20
  part access   road tarmac      w 10..14 from carpark
  part fence    fence            h 3..4 round 80..150
