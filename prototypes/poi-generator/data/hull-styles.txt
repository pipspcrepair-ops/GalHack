# How each faction builds a hull (src/Gen/HullStyle.cs): one rule set for every generated vessel and station, these its
# numbers. DESIGN, "Ships and structures by faction": a faction reads by its silhouette at long range before colour or
# detail, so the shape rules come first and differ most. Counts are per 100 m of hull; length and beam are shares of the
# size class's; colours are hex. Husk ships are any faction's hull hijacked (the Infested state), not a style of their own.
#
#   length L / beam B          proportions
#   bow taper|round|blunt|needle|ram
#   shoulder S                 the shoulders' chamfer, a share of the hull's height (small: a block)
#   spine H [W]                dorsal spine height and half width, m
#   segments N DEPTH           waists between N+1 segments, taking DEPTH of the beam in
#   ribs EVERY [OUT]           frames round the hull every EVERY m, standing OUT m proud
#   lattice yes|no             a truss down the spine
#   pods N / bolted N / spikes N    per 100 m: cargo pod stacks, bolted-on modules, spikes
#   plates SHARE               mismatched plates over this share of the shell
#   fins N                     swept fins, built in short straight runs (the 1 m grid's curve)
#   symmetric yes|no           everything added mirrored port and starboard
#   tower-offset S             the tower shifted to starboard, a share of its half width
#   engines MIN MAX LENGTH HEIGHT   nacelles, their length against the default, their height a share of the hull's
#   hull / trim / band A [B] / dark / pattern stripes|chevrons|checker|hazard|none / grime 0-3
#   glow COLOUR|none           glowing strips; numbers yes|no: stencilled hull numbers
#   cargo / scrap C...         pods' colours (colour-coded), mismatched plates' and modules' colours
