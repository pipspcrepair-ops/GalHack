# Places below ground: what each purpose asks of its plan. None of this is a layout. Each purpose names
# the shared plan parts it draws on and how much, its sizes and rooms, the zones it is parted into and what
# a century of neglect does to it; the planner (src/Gen/Arch/Depths*.cs) rolls a fresh plan from these rules,
# and the place's builders (their tech level, and their habit: orderly, radial, linear, warren, or a
# faction's own) and its world's age and fall tilt every weight.
#
#   depth <purpose>
#     levels a b         plan levels below ground (a rare place goes one deeper)
#     first a b          blocks (2 m) from the surface down to the first level's floor
#     rock a b           extra blocks of rock between levels
#     head <part w>...   what shows above ground: hood (a blockhouse with vents and a mast), hatch (a small
#                        one), portal (a low bermed entrance), headframe (a shaft house), maw (an alien dome)
#     down <part w>...   the way from the head to the first level: straight (a long stair), ramp, ladder
#     between <part w>.. the ways between levels that don't share a core: straight, ramp, ladder
#     stacked p          chance the levels share one core straight down (a switchback stairwell or a
#                        ladderway, a lift beside it at higher tech)
#     lift p             chance of that lift
#     main w0 w1 h0 h1   the main ways, in blocks (2 m): width and height (big facilities 4 by 3: 8 by 6 m)
#     side w0 w1 h0 h1   the lesser ways
#     room h0 h1         room heights
#     plan <band> <part w>...  the parts a level may be: spine, hub, ring, pods (a ring of pod halls), grid,
#                        hall (a hall with a gallery over it), chamber (a round chamber with a gallery round
#                        it), gallery (alcoves), cells (cell blocks round a guarded hub), drifts, organic.
#                        Bands: top, upper, deep, deepest, any.
#     span <part> p      a structure through every level: chamber (a silo's shaft), shaft (a mine's)
#     rooms <band> <room>[!][*a-b][?]...   as in programs.txt; fill <room>...
#     zones <zone>...    zones in order from the way in; lock door|blast|airlock|decon parts them
#     crawl p            service crawlways (2 m) beside the main ways
#     blast p            blast vestibules between sections of one zone
#     ends a b           dead ends, each with a vault, a hidden room or nothing behind it
#     secrets a b        hidden rooms behind weak walls
#     raw p              share of spaces cut in raw rock
#     alien              round chambers, curved ways, ramps
#     lining <paint>     wall, steel, timber, ice
#     feature ops        an operations hall with a gallery over it on one of the levels
#     size m             the least it spans across, in metres: a large place reaches out with another wing until it does
#     age <kind p>...    collapse (a corridor's roof down, a way dug round it), cave (broken into), squat (a
#                        squatters' tunnel), sealed (a door welded, the section reached another way), breach
#                        (the ground fallen in from the surface): their chance a century after the fall; the
#                        world's age and how violently it fell scale them
