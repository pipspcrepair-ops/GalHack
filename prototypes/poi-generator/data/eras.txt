#   era <low|mid|high>

#                                                wall panel plinth floor roof steel rust pipe pipehot accent grating
#     paint <name> <rgb>                         hazard black glass lamp emergency (the large places' paints)
#     swap <material> <material>[.<finish>]:<w> ...
#                                                what the made materials of a place built from a style kit become in
#                                                this era: one pick per place, so a place is all of a piece
#     roofing <material>.<finish>:<w> ...        what its roofs are covered in (pitched ones and flat)
#     tone <material> <rgb>|<rgb>...             the colours a swapped material takes (one per place, shaded as built)
#     keep <block types>                         what is left as built (machines, tanks and pumps: the diesel-age bits)
#     windows <share>                            the share of a place's window columns kept; the rest are walled in
#     ribbon <share>                             the chance a window widens along its wall into a band of glass
#     gable <metres>                             flat roofs up to this span take a pitched roof (0: none)
#     chamfer <yes|no>                           its buildings' outer corners cut back on the diagonal
#     edge <square|round>                        a flat roof's edge: square, or rounded off along the wall top
#     strips <rgb>|none                          a light strip along each floor line and the top of its outer walls
#     lamp <rgb>                                 what its lamps glow
#     vehicles <carts|motors|grav>               what stands abandoned in its yards
