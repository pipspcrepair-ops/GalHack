# What places' faces are finished in: the surface the place shader draws over a block's colour.
#
#   default <material> <role> <side>:<finish> ...    what a face is, by what it is made of and the part it plays
#   room <purpose> ... floor:<f> wall:<f> ceiling:<f> a room's own finishes inside (over the built materials)
#   built <material> ...                             the materials a room's own finish may go over
#
# Roles: wall, floor, roof, decor (falls back to wall), rock, any. Sides: out and in (a wall's outside and
# inside), floor, ceiling, roof (anything facing the sky: a roof, a yard's paving, a flat top), soffit (an
# eave's underside), all. A finish may name its small choice after a dot (brick.flemish, tile.checker);
# left out, each place picks its own, the same all over it. A side may list alternatives with a condition
# each, the last one unconditional: "painted=render/poured" is render on a tinted block, poured concrete
# on a grey one. Conditions: painted, grey, wood, straw, dark, light, slope, flat, low, mid, high, alien.
# A kit's own "finish" lines (same form, no "default") come first; a block's own finish (Block.Variant,
# set by a builder that chose it, such as a house's cladding) comes before both.
#
# Finishes: plain brick render plaster weatherboard logs stone rubble poured precast block corrugated seam
# plate planks parquet tile lino carpet rooftile slate shingle thatch tar walltile deck hull paving chitin
# resin bone fused basalt bronze rockface soil ceilingtile armour patched cloth ash
