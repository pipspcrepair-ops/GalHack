# Building programs: what a building is for, so its plan follows it. A program is a recipe of
# shared rooms (data/rooms.txt) by band of storeys, plus its massing; a style kit re-skins it.
#
#   program <id>
#     style        the kit whose palette builds it (concrete, clinic, ice, mine, meridian...)
#     width a b    length along the spine; depth a b across it (16 or more)
#     floors a b   storeys at and above ground; levels a b, storeys below ground
#     surface      full (the building stands) or hut (only a stair house shows above ground)
#     stair        switch (a switchback stairwell) or ladder (a ladderway shaft)
#     lift p       chance of a lift core beside the stair
#     roof         flat, access (the stair goes on up to the roof), pad (and a landing pad)
#     windows p    share of the outer wall's middle row glazed, above ground
#     cavern p     chance the deepest storey breaks into a natural cave
#     breach p     chance of a collapse breach from the surface into the top basement (hut only)
#     secrets a b  hidden rooms behind breakable walls
#     hall <band> <room>   on those storeys, one side of the spine is a single great room
#     rooms <band> <room>[!][*min-max][?] ...   ! a must on every such storey, ? optional
#         bands: ground, upper (between ground and top), top, above (every storey above ground),
#                surface (ground and above), basement, shallow (sublevel 1), deep (lower half),
#                deepest (the lowest storey), any
#     fill         rooms that fill whatever is left
